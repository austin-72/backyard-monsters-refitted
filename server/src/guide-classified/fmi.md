The cold didn't go away for good. Every month it comes back for the players who beat Hell Freezes Over's last wave, and only they can read this chapter.

## When {#fmi-when}

- **Every month from the 15th, for 7 days** (from 00:00 UTC on the 15th to 00:00 UTC on the 22nd).
- **Two invasions take turns:** Frozen Monster Invasion I in October, II in November, I in December, and so on.
- **Its button** sits in the workers' column, where Moloch's Gauntlet's goes, while the invasion is on and from a week before. It opens the invasion's window: how far you've held it off, the next wave's attackers, the totem, and **Fight**.

## The waves {#fmi-waves}

- **32 waves** for each invasion, fought in order in your main yard, as often as it takes: there's no limit on tries.
- **Who comes:** Hell Freezes Over's ice cretins, and the Inferno's champions with them: Korath, Drull, Ashkarr and Rimegrave. A big group comes as a stream, not as one crowd.
- **Your Compound is frozen shut** while a wave is on, as in Hell Freezes Over: your towers, walls and traps hold the yard. Half a minute after the last surge, the blizzard finishes off whoever is left, so what counts is what a wave destroys before then.
- **Won:** every attacker gone (the Permafrost Hulks' Shivlings too) with your yard standing. **Lost:** 99% of your yard destroyed, or Surrender.
- Nothing is repaired for free: damaged buildings start their usual repair afterwards.
- Your progress belongs to that month's invasion, and starts again with the next one.

## The totems {#fmi-totems}

- **Each invasion has its own totem.** Win wave 1 for it, and it rises at waves 10, 20, 30, 31 and 32, up to level 6.
- **It's yours for good,** at the highest level you've reached in any month: a decoration for your yard, in your storage until you place it, shown in the D.A.V.E. Club's Trophies tab and as an icon by your name in chat and in your alliance's Members list.
- The invasions pay no Shiny: the totems are the prize. Holding off all 32 waves is announced in Global chat.
