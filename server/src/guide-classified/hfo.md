You've seen it: something is wrong in the Inferno. This is a one-time story in your main yard, and it came to you because every monster on Strongbox pages 1 to 5 is unlocked. Only players it has come to can read this chapter.

## Three days of ice {#hfo-days}

- **Day 1, "Something's not right":** small ice patches appear, 6 at first, then one more every 2 hours, 12 in all. Send a worker to clear each one; your workers have a thing or two to say about it.
- **Day 2, "It's the curse":** 5 big patches that won't break. Send a worker to try each one: it cracks and holds. They stay until the curse breaks.
- **Day 3, "Hell has frozen over":** the ground freezes and every defence tower is sealed in ice, with a counter of how many are freed. Free them all with your workers (in a fight the towers still fire as usual). A large block of ice stands just off your yard's right-hand corner from now on. The last tower freed opens the waves.
- A new day begins no sooner than a day after the one before, and only once that day's work is done. It starts when you load your yard, or while you're in it.
- **Break all ice:** while the ice is in your yard, a button sends your idle workers to every patch and sealed tower that can be worked.

## The waves {#hfo-waves}

- **13 waves of ice cretins,** fought in your main yard: Shivlings, Slushguts, Rimeclaws, Sleetwings, Hailspitters and Permafrost Hulks, which burst into Shivlings. The waves are set for an Under Hall 6 yard: waves 1 to 4 come from one side, 5 to 9 from two and 10 to 13 from all four.
- **Your Compound is frozen solid** while a wave is on: none of its monsters comes out to fight. Your towers, walls and traps hold the yard. A while after the last surge, the ice cretins still standing start to melt, so no wave drags on.
- **Won:** every ice cretin gone. **Lost:** 99% of your yard destroyed, or Surrender.
- **3 tries a wave,** counted when it starts. A win within them pays 5 Shiny (wave 13 pays 155), once: 215 Shiny in all. All 3 lost: the wave is skipped and the next one opens. A skipped wave can be fought again at any time, with no tries counted and no Shiny.
- Nothing is repaired for free: damaged buildings start their usual repair afterwards, and monsters lost defending stay lost. Wild attacks wait while a wave is on.

## The reward {#hfo-reward}

- **Win all 13 waves:** the ground thaws, the big patches melt, the ice tomb bursts, and **Rimegrave, the ice champion,** stands there. Unlock him on Strongbox page 5 (after Ashkarr) and train him in the Academy up to level 6, for what the other champions cost. He takes 350 housing, his hits ice towers over and freeze monsters, and his look changes with his Academy level. The champion skins in the D.A.V.E. Club are for him too.
- **Winning the last wave also brings:** the **Frozen Hell** ground in the D.A.V.E. Club, a **frozen look** for Korath and Drull (the Club's Champions tab), and the six ice cretins as **pets**.
- And something more comes back every month after that. Win the last wave, and this guide will tell you about it.
