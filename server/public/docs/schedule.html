When things happen

Almost everything runs on UTC (London's winter time, also called GMT). The one exception is the Wart Bloom, which follows US Central time (Chicago).

Every day, at midnight UTC

WhatWhat happens at the new day
Daily RewardA new reward can be collected from the popup on your main yard. Collect every day to keep your streak; missing a whole UTC day starts it again at day 1.
Daily questsThree new daily quests in the Quest Book. Yesterday's quests that you finished but didn't collect can still be collected for one more day.
Moloch's FavorA new free Magma Slots spin in the Brimstone Pit.
Siege: the DailyA new yard and army in the Devil's Arcade, the same for everyone, with three tries a day.
Valgos Golf: the DailyA new course of nine holes, the same for everyone, with its own board for the day.
Hellgate Defense 2: the Daily ChallengeA new map and two modifiers, with their own board.

Every week

The Wart Bloom: every weekend

From Friday 6 pm until Sunday midnight, US Central time (daylight saving included): 54 hours. Warts grow 3 times as fast in your main yard (not in outposts), and the time you're offline counts too.

In UTC that is Friday 23:00 to Monday 05:00 while the US is on daylight saving time (from the second Sunday in March to the first Sunday in November), and Saturday 00:00 to Monday 06:00 the rest of the year. Its start and end are announced in Global chat, and the game shows when it ends in your own time.

Balthazar's Flight: a new course every Monday

The Devil's Arcade flyer has one course for everyone from Monday 00:00 UTC to the end of Sunday, with a board for the week.

Every month

Moloch's Gauntlet: the first 7 days of each month

Open from 00:00 UTC on the 1st until 00:00 UTC on the 8th, played from your main yard. Its button shows from 7 days before it opens. Progress starts again on the 1st of every month, so every stage's reward can be won again; admins can open or close it early.

Once a year

Trick or Treat: 30 October to 2 November

Open from midnight on 30 October until midnight at the end of 2 November, US Central time (05:00 UTC on 30 October to 06:00 UTC on 3 November), from the button under Moloch's Gauntlet's on your main yard. Its button shows from 3 days before. A trick you still have to face comes when you're home, even after it ends. Everyone who knocked on a door gets 6 Mini-Pumpkins the first time they come home after it ends (within 30 days).
