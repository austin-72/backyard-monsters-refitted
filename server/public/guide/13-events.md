# Events and rewards {#events}

Almost everything runs on UTC; the Wart Bloom follows US Central time. [When things happen](/schedule) has every time and time zone, and **/events** in chat opens a calendar of every event and daily reset with its countdown in your own time and a Go button.

## Daily Reward {#daily-reward}

- **10 Shiny every day you collect;** 100 on every 7th day of a streak and 200 on every 14th. Keep collecting every day (UTC) and the streak keeps growing; missing a whole day starts it again at day 1.
- The Daily Reward button at the top (where the gift button was) shows your streak day, and spins while today's reward is waiting. Once today's is collected, its window says when the next one comes, in your own time.

## Invite your friends {#invites}

- **Your invite link** is in the Invite Friends window (the top bar's Invite button), with a Copy button. It opens the sign-up form with your name and the Shiny you both get.
- **When a friend signs up with it, you both get 250 Shiny,** paid when their yard is made: once per friend, and never between accounts on the same connection (see the [Rules](rules.md#invites)).
- A friend who joins through your link gets their yard near yours, and an invite into your alliance if you lead it or are an officer.
- The window says how many friends have joined through your link, how many earned the Shiny, and how many haven't played yet.
- **Invite-only looks** in the D.A.V.E. Club, for 1, 2 and 3 friends: the grounds Ashfall, Obsidian Glass and the Soulfire Rift, and the chat name effects Ember Glow, Brimstone and Soulfire.

## The Wart Bloom {#wart-bloom}

- **Every weekend, from Friday 6 pm until Sunday midnight (US Central time),** warts grow three times as fast in your main yard: one every 1.6 hours, up to 20. Never in outposts.
- Offline time counts, and while your yard is open new warts keep popping up.
- Global chat says when the bloom starts and how long it lasts. While it's on, a chip under the top bar says how long is left (click it for the end in your own time) and how many warts are in your yard, and warns when the yard is full, since a full yard wastes the bloom's growth.

## Moloch's Gauntlet {#gauntlet}

- **Every month, for the first 7 days (UTC):** a ladder of 13 Moloch yards fought in order, from your main yard. Your progress is your own and starts again each month.
- **Win at 90% destroyed.** Damage stays between tries; you get 3 tries per gate, then the yard heals and that gate's reward is lost for the month (you still have to beat it to go on).
- **Rewards:** 5 Shiny per gate and 150 more for the last (up to 215 a month), plus resources rising to 30 million bone, coal and sulfur and 15 million magma at gate 13. No loot from the yards themselves.
- **The Gauntlet's button** sits in the workers' column while it's open and in the 3 days before; its tip says when it opens or closes, in your own time, and the window counts down. The first visit home after it opens, and once on its last day, the game reminds you, with how many gates are left.
- **The window** shows the gate to fight and its prize, the army you'd attack with and a map of the gate's layout, and this month's Shiny: claimed, still to win and lost. After an attack it says how much of the gate you destroyed, how much breaks it, the attempts left and the prize still waiting.
- Catapult shots cost their resources in the Gauntlet, as in any attack, and End Attack reminds you it uses an attempt.

## Trick or Treat {#trick-or-treat}

- **Every Halloween, 30 October to 2 November (US Central time):** the jack-o'-lantern button under Moloch's Gauntlet's opens a door for each of the five wild tribes: the Hellionnaires, the Kozmodeus, the Abaddonakki, the Beelzenauts and Moloch's horde. Knock once on each.
- **Behind each door, an even chance:** a treat of **25 Shiny**, paid at once, or a **trick**: **1,000 level 6 Zagnoids** appear all at once in a ring round your main yard and attack it.
- **A trick is a real raid.** It takes real loot, and your buildings mend as after any raid. Destroy every last Zagnoid and you get the 25 Shiny after all; if your Town Hall falls, they all go home with what they took.
- **Face a trick before knocking on the next door.** If you leave during its countdown, it comes again the next time you're home; leaving during the fight loses it.
- The button's ring turns while a door waits; **/events** in chat and the calendar show how long is left. Global chat hears about every trick, and every trick beaten; each trick has a replay in your Attack Log.
- **When it's over, everyone who knocked gets 6 Mini-Pumpkins** for their yard, in storage (Buildings, Decorations), the first time they come home.

## Cobwebs for Halloween {#cobwebs}

- **From 20 October every yard grows spooky:** cobwebs spun in the gaps of buildings and strung between neighbouring ones (never to walls, decorations or traps; decorations have none at all), dusty roofs, spiders and a gathering gloom.
- **They build up every day to Halloween** (a quarter of them on 20 October, all of them on 31 October), then clear away by 3 November (US Central time).
- They go with **Light and shadows** in the settings: turn that off and the cobwebs go too (see [Your yard](03-yard.md#light)).

## Trophies {#trophies}

- **The Gauntlet Trophy:** break all 13 gates. **The Purist Trophy:** do it in one month without firing a single catapult (the game asks before you fire one while it can still be won).
- Trophies are yours for good, as decorations for your yard: the message for a trophy you won has **Place it now**. The D.A.V.E. Club's Trophies tab shows what you've won and how to win the rest.
- Small trophy icons show next to players' names in the chat's name menu and the alliance's Members list.

## Milestones in chat {#milestones}

- Global chat announces news from the admins (gold), big Brimstone Pit wins and jackpots (purple), and milestones like conquering Moloch's Gauntlet (light blue).
