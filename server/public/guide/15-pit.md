# The Brimstone Pit {#pit}

Build the Brimstone Pit (one per yard, from Under Hall 1) and play against the house for Shiny. Every result is drawn by the server, every game is set to pay back a little under 100% of what is bet, and Shiny can never be bought, cashed out or traded. Higher Pit levels open more games.

## The games {#pit-games}

| Game | Pit level | How it plays |
|---|:-:|---|
| Magma Drop | 1 | A Spurtz bounces down 10 rows of pegs into a cup (low, medium or high risk). Drop up to 20 at once. |
| Brimstone Scratchers | 1 | Tickets of 5, 25 or 100 Shiny you scratch with the mouse. |
| Higher or Lower | 1 | Will the next card be higher or lower? Every right guess raises your multiplier by its odds; cash out whenever you like, or lose it all on a wrong guess. Skip a card you don't like. |
| Wormzer Roulette | 2 | 29 segments: monsters, Lava / Ash, King Wormzer. |
| Bone Pile | 2 | 25 piles hiding Sabnox; cash out whenever you like (reaching 1000x cashes you out). |
| Cinderjack | 2 | Blackjack against Moloch: hit, stand, double or split; blackjack pays 3 to 2 and Moloch stands on 17. |
| Magma Slots | 3 | Three reels; three King Wormzer win the jackpot. |
| Devil's Dice | 3 | Up to four players at a table bet before the Devil throws. Every throw without his face multiplies your bet; bank before the next throw, or he takes everything still on the table. |
| The Undertaker's Offer | 3 | Twelve sealed coffins, twelve prizes from nothing to 5x your bet. Keep one, open the others, and after every round the Undertaker offers to buy yours: deal or no deal? |
| Balthazar's Ascent | 4 | Everyone plays the same round: cash out before a Sharpshooter Tower shoots him down. |
| Magma Derby | 5 | A race every five minutes; bet on six monsters. |
| Korath's Fortune | 6 | Korath's machine: the champions on five lines (rows and diagonals), each a fifth of your bet; three Korath win the slots' jackpot. |

Magma Drop, Roulette, Bone Pile, Ascent, Devil's Dice and the Derby have a **?** button by LOBBY with their rules and what they pay.

Every game has a small **(i)** at its top left with its odds: what it pays back over time, how often a play pays more than the bet, and each result with what it pays and its chance. They are worked out from the rules the game is played by, without luck (see Fairness).

## How it's played {#pit-play}

- **Fairness:** the Fairness tab shows the seeds every result is drawn from and how a bet's numbers are made from them. You choose your own client seed; changing seeds waits until a Bone Pile, Higher or Lower, Undertaker's Offer or Cinderjack game you have open is finished.
- **The jackpot** grows with every spin of either slot machine, and both pay it. Every jackpot is announced in chat.
- **Moloch's Favor** is a free 10 Shiny Magma Slots spin every day (UTC). It's a real spin and can win the jackpot; the lobby counts down to the next one.
- **Bets:** chips you can't afford are dimmed, and **MAX** bets the most you can afford (in Roulette and the Derby it counts what's already on your slip). A bet of 100 Shiny or more that is over half your Shiny asks first. Each game keeps the bet you last used, Magma Drop its risk, and the auto cash-out and auto bank boxes what you typed.
- **AUTO** (the slots, Magma Drop and Roulette) plays a number of rounds and can stop after so many, when the run is down by an amount, or on a big win. On the slots, click SPIN, the lever or the reels while they spin to stop on the result at once. **QUICK** shortens the animation in Magma Drop, Roulette, the slots and Scratchers.
- **Ascent and Devil's Dice** take a bet while a round is running, for the next one (click it to cancel). A bet on Devil's Dice, the Derby or Ascent whose round hasn't started can be **taken back**.
- **Cinderjack's HINT** shows the basic-strategy move for your hand (off until you turn it on).
- **The Derby** shows who's betting on the race and how much is on each runner, and PAST RACES lists the last winners, their odds and who came second.
- **Big wins** of 10 times your bet or more fill the window: BIG, MEGA (50x) and EPIC (200x) WIN. Wins paying more than 500 Shiny are announced in chat.
- **History** lists every bet you've made, with a game filter, pages and OLDER; click a bet for its details. It also shows what you have bet, been paid and won or lost today (the Pit's day turns at midnight UTC).
- **The Live tab** shows everyone's bets as they happen, today's 10 biggest wins and the last jackpots.
- A Bone Pile game left for a day is cashed out or refunded for you, and a hand of Cinderjack left open waits for you.
- **Keys:** Space presses the game's main button (Deal, Start, Bet, Drop, Spin, Buy, Place bet, never Cash out). In Cinderjack H, S, D and P are Hit, Stand, Double and Split; in Higher or Lower the up and down arrows are Higher and Lower.
- A speaker button turns the Pit's sound off and on, and while the Pit is open you are never marked away.
