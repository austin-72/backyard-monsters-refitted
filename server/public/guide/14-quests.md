# The Quest Book {#quests}

The Quests button (or Q in your yard) opens the Quest Book: 286 quests in eleven categories, each drawn as a tree. Finish a quest, collect its reward, and the quests under it open. The book opens in your own yard.

## How it works {#how-it-works}

- **Eleven categories:** Start, Yard, Monsters, Battles, Map Room, Outposts, Alliances, Social, Events, the Devil's Arcade and the Brimstone Pit. Each shows how many quests you've collected and has a badge for the ones ready.
- **A tree of quests:** a quest opens once you collect the one above it; a locked quest's "Opens when you collect: ..." is a link to that quest. Drag or scroll to look around the tree; "Hide finished" folds away branches you've completed.
- **What you've already done counts.** Your building levels, the monsters you've unlocked, your outposts, your alliance and more are ready to collect straight away.
- **Go there** takes you where the quest is done: the building (or the build menu, opened at it), a wart, your highest tower, the fullest harvester, the mailbox, the map, the chat, the Leaderboards, the Gauntlet, the Brimstone Pit, the Devil's Arcade, your outposts or the right tab of the Alliances window. A window opened from the book offers a way back to it.
- **Collect all** collects everything that's ready, keeps going down the trees, and opens any chests along the way.
- **Sealed quests:** a few quests show only as a sealed node, with no task and no reward, until you do the thing that reveals them; a notice says when one opens up. They are optional, and once collected they stay shown. The last of them is Master of the book.

## Rewards {#quest-rewards}

- **Resources and Shiny,** paid straight into your main yard. The whole book pays about 1,500 Shiny: about 930 from its quests and 585 from its chests. Its top line says how much Shiny you can still earn from it.
- **A chest in every category but the Brimstone Pit** opens once you've collected all its quests (optional ones don't count). Open every chest, the Devil's Arcade's included (it pays resources only), for the book's own: 25 million each of bone, coal and sulfur, 12.5 million magma and 150 Shiny.
- **Optional quests** are the ones not every player can do, or that come down to luck: recruiting and pinning a note (alliance leaders and officers), setting a relationship (the leader), helping a power-up, being in your world's top three alliances, bringing in friends, putting up a Sign, the slots jackpot and the sealed ones. They pay as usual but aren't needed for a chest.
- **The Brimstone Pit's and the Devil's Arcade's quests pay resources only,** so playing for a quest never wins you Shiny.

## Daily quests {#daily-quests}

- **Three a day,** different for every player and new at midnight UTC (the book says when that is in your own time, and its countdown ticks every second). They are always ones your yard can do: a new yard may get fewer until it has a Flinger, an Incubator and so on, and "Hold off the wild" only comes when a raid can still arrive before the day ends. Each pays resources and 1 Shiny.
- **Finish them all** for a bonus of 500,000 each of bone, coal and sulfur, 250,000 magma and 2 Shiny.
- **A free reroll** once a day swaps one of your daily quests for another.
- **Yesterday's** daily quests that you finished but didn't collect stay in the book as "Yesterday:" for a day.

## Keeping track {#tracking}

- **The quest dock** at the bottom right shows every quest ready to collect, each with its own Collect button (a click anywhere on its row's height), then up to 3 quests you chose to **track** (marked with a flag), then the ones closest to done, with their progress bars. With two or more ready it shows **Collect all**. Click a row to open the book at that quest; click its title bar to fold it to one line.
- The Quests button shows how many quests are ready.
- A notice slides in at the top when a quest is ready, with its reward and its own Collect; click it to go to the quest. Collecting says what you got.
- The original game's quest list is still there: "The original game's quest list" at the bottom of the book.
