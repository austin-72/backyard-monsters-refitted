# Monsters {#monsters}

The Inferno roster grew from eight monsters to sixteen.

## Champions and Rezghul {#champions}

- **Korath, Drull and Ashkarr are monsters you hatch,** not champions in a cage: unlock them on Strongbox page 5, hatch them with magma, house them in the Compound and fling them like any other. Housing: Korath 400, Drull 400, Ashkarr 350.
- Korath burns what he hits, throws fireballs at flyers from level 4 and stomps from level 5.
- **Rezghul in the Inferno:** Strongbox page 4 after King Wormzer, 500,000 magma to hatch, 200 housing, trained up to level 6. He raises your side's fallen monsters during a battle, with 75% of their health (champions 25%).
- **Unlocked the fair way.** Korath, Drull, Rezghul and Ashkarr have to be unlocked in the Strongbox like everyone else.
- Every Inferno monster trains to level 6 in a level 5 Academy. Champions list the abilities they get at each level.
- The Quest Book's "Something in the Ashes" says something has been watching you fight: win battle after battle, and in time it may decide to fight at your side.

| Monster | Where | What it does |

| Fusebug | Strongbox page 1, housing 10 | Runs at your defences, creeps into the building and explodes. |

## Balance {#monster-balance}

- Small monsters grow stronger at levels 4 to 6: Spurtz, Zagnoid, Fusebug, Malphus, Clinkerjaw and Flickerfiend.
- Toned down: King Wormzer, Valgos and Balthazar at low levels, and the Emberghoul's healing.
- Sabnox takes less room (65) and has more health; Ashkarr has more health.
- Valgos is 1.5 times as fast (speed 3 at every level). King Wormzer's splash when he pops up from underground reaches half as far (50).
- Walls stop Balthazar: he still flies, but goes round walls or breaks through them, as walking monsters do.

## Knowing your monsters {#info}

- **Padlocks** on monsters you haven't unlocked, in every monster menu. The Strongbox says what an unlock takes ("Lv 2 · 38.4K Sulfur", red where you're short) and opens on the next monster to unlock.
- **Damage per second** in the Strongbox, the Academy and the incubators, worked out from how often a monster really hits, and its health and damage **per 100 housing**, to compare monsters of different sizes.
- **A tag line** in monster info: what it goes for, whether it flies, burrows or jumps walls (Balthazar: "Stopped by walls"), and its reach. The attack bar's tips show health and damage per second too.
- The Academy says "Needs Academy level N" and still shows the training's cost and time, and what it's busy with and for how long; its upgrade says what level monsters it can then train to.
- Clicking a locked monster in the Incubator says it isn't unlocked yet, with Open the Strongbox; not enough magma says how much it costs and what you have, with Top up. The window shows the Compound's room, with what's queued.

## Your army {#army}

- **Refill army:** the Compound remembers your last full army and queues what's missing in your incubators, telling you the magma first.
- **Extra Housing ending:** an hour before, you're told how much too little room the Compound will have. If monsters must go, they go one at a time from the kind you have most of, and you're told which.
- Monsters trained in the Academy show their new health as soon as the training finishes.
- Monsters move between your own yards and outposts only (see [Outposts](08-outposts.md#transfers)).
