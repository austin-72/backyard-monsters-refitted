# Buildings and towers {#buildings}

Two towers, the Brimstone Pit and the Devil's Arcade were added, the Strongbox and Academy got a fifth level, and the buildings that still looked like the overworld got Inferno art.

## New towers {#new-towers}

- **Cinder Coil.** From Under Hall 3, 8 levels. Every second it spins up and shocks a monster, and the arc leaps on to up to 8 more nearby, a little weaker each time. Hits ground and air.
- **Obsidian Mortar.** From Under Hall 3, 8 levels, long range. Lobs a shell to where its target stood: everything in the blast is hit, hardest in the middle. It can't hit monsters right next to it.

## New buildings and levels {#new-levels}

- **More tower levels** (the new ones need Under Hall 6): the Sharpshooter and the Blast Tower go to level 10, like the overworld's Sniper and Cannon Towers; the Quake Tower, the Magma Tower, the Cinder Coil and the Obsidian Mortar go to level 8. The Quake Tower's damage was spread over its 8 levels: level 8 hits as hard as level 6 used to.
- **Strongbox level 5** (needs Under Hall 5): opens page 5, the champions.
- **Infernal Academy level 5:** trains every Inferno monster up to level 6.
- **The Brimstone Pit,** the casino: one per yard from Under Hall 1, up to level 6 (see [The Brimstone Pit](15-pit.md)).
- **The Devil's Arcade,** Moloch's hall of games: one in your main yard, cheap and quick to build (see [The Devil's Arcade](16-arcade.md)).
- **The Catapult** from Under Hall 3 in your main yard, with Inferno ammunition (see [Battles and the Catapult](11-battles.md#catapult)).
- **Signs** from the D.A.V.E. Club (see [The D.A.V.E. Club](17-club.md#signs)).

## How many you can build {#limits}

- **More bone blocks in your main yard:** 10 at Under Hall 1, 50 at 2, 90 at 3, 160 at 4, 260 at 5 and 300 at 6 (the stock game has 0, 30, 60, 120, 200 and 220).
- **A fourth Magma Tower** at Under Hall 6 (the stock game has 3).
- **Outposts have their own limits:** 4 of each harvester, 1 Flinger, 1 Yard Planner, 2 hatcheries, 200 blocks, 40 traps, 4 of each tower, 2 Cinder Coils, 2 Obsidian Mortars, 1 Compound, 1 Incubation Control Station and 1 Juicer.
- In the build menu, a building at its limit says when the Under Hall lets you build more ("+1 at Under Hall 5").
- Yard Planner and decorations can be built in your main yard and in outposts (a few that don't suit the Inferno are left out).

## The build menu and building panels {#build-menu}

- The build menu shows each building as it stands in your yard; towers turn towards your mouse and animated buildings move. Locked buildings are dark silhouettes.
- A requirement you haven't met (underlined in red, "Level 4 Under Hall") opens that building's upgrade when clicked. "You need a General Store" and "You need a Map Room" have a **Build it** button.
- A building's upgrade tab says how much health the next level has; its **Info** button (it was "More...") shows its health now.
- Towers show their damage as per shot × shots a second = damage per second, and their range in cells.
- Clicking matches what you see: you click a building by its picture, and the one drawn on top gets the click. The building under your mouse glows.
- Recycle and stop-building questions name the building and its level and say what comes back. Questions that can't be undone have a **Keep** button that only closes, and the action in red, which ignores clicks for its first moment so a double click can't go straight through.

## Walls, blocks and traps {#walls}

- **Blocks by the dozen:** pick how many in the build window (up to your limit), then click the yard for each one; each is built at once. Esc stops.
- **More walls:** 20 more at every Under Hall level: 30, 70, 110, 180, 280 and 320.
- **Monsters go round walls:** they walk twice as far round a wall before they choose to break through it.
- **Drag a line of walls:** with a wall in hand, drag on the yard to lay a straight line, each paid as it goes down (Shift-drag still scrolls).
- **Upgrade all walls** (the build menu's Defensive tab, or a wall's menu): every wall goes up to the level you pick, paid with resources (exactly what they still cost) or with Shiny.
- **Traps several at a time:** pick how many, then click the yard for each; each is built the moment it's placed, with no worker. You only pay for the ones you placed.
- **Traps that went off stay** in your yard, faded and disarmed. **Re-arm all traps** (the Defensive tab, or a disarmed trap) sets them all again, for their build costs.

## Decorations {#decorations}

- Decorations are built the moment you place them. After placing one from storage, the next one stays in your hand while more are stored (Esc stops); **Place all** on a stored decoration puts every one down next to the Under Hall.
- **Animated decorations** (Decorations, Animated): a Bubbling Cauldron, a Chained Imp, a Lava Fountain, a Soul Lantern, a Hellfire Brazier, a Skull Totem, a Pendulum Blade, an Ember Geyser, the Eye of the Abyss, a Spit Roast, a Bat Weathervane, a Magma Waterwheel and a Tentacle Pit, 150 to 400 Shiny each (prices on the [Shiny page](/shiny)).
- Trophies you win are decorations too (see [Events and rewards](13-events.md#trophies)).

## Inferno art {#art}

- Remade: the General Store, Incubation Control Station, Flinger, Catapult, Map Room, Monster Juicer, Yard Planner, the outpost hall, the Monster Locker and the Academy's training animations.
- Resource buildings change look as they level up, all the way to level 10.
- The Under Hall is 130 x 130, the same size as the Town Hall, and sits in the middle of its square.

## Hatching, the Strongbox and the Academy {#hatching}

- **The Incubator:** hold a monster down to keep adding it until the queue is full or the magma runs out; Shift-click queues as many as there's room and magma for. Hold a queued monster to keep removing it.
- **The Incubation Control Station** (was the Hatchery Control Center; its button says Open ICS): four monsters across, an orange Magma bar, and small arrows under the queue to move a monster left or right.
- **The Strongbox** unlocks one monster at a time and can't be upgraded while unlocking. Locked monsters show the level and Sulfur they need, or "Can start", and an unlock in progress shows its countdown over the building.
- **Each Academy trains one monster at a time** (two Academies, two trainings) and can't be upgraded while training. Its Overview shows every monster's level and the next level's time.
- **The Monster Juicer** turns Inferno monsters into magma (60 / 80 / 100% of their hatch cost by level). It asks first, names any champions in the batch, and Select All leaves champions out (hold Shift to include them). Shift-click picks all of one kind, Ctrl-click (or Alt-click) takes one off.
