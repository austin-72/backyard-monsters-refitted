# Signing in, settings and the interface {#interface}

## Signing in {#signing-in}

- **The login page** leads with "Inferno Map Room 2 · a custom server". The first time you open the game in a browser, you choose between Create account and I have an account. "What's different?" in the corner opens this guide.
- **Sign in with your email or your username,** in any capitals. Tab moves between the fields, Enter goes to the next field or signs you in, an eye shows what you typed, and you're told when Caps Lock is on. While the server is asked, the button says "Logging in…".
- **Saved accounts** ("WHO'S PLAYING?"): up to 5 accounts are remembered in this browser. Click a name to sign straight in without the password, for 14 days after you last played on that account. After that, or once a password change or a sign-out ended it, the page says why and asks for the password. The × forgets an account (with Undo for a few seconds).
- **Keep me signed in on this computer:** the next visit opens your yard straight away. Untick it on a shared computer. A login belongs to the browser it was made in, so a VPN or a switch between Wi-Fi and mobile data doesn't sign you out, and a server restart doesn't either.
- **One place at a time:** an account plays in one place at a time. Signing in while it is already playing somewhere asks first: Play here signs the other one out (it's told where and when), No leaves it playing. If that wasn't you, sign in again and change your password.
- **Your language** is picked on the login page (English, French, Spanish or Portuguese), kept for your next visit, and a first visit opens in your browser's language when the game has it. This guide is in English.

## Creating an account {#sign-up}

- **The name:** 2 to 12 letters, numbers or \_. **The password:** 8 or more characters, one of them a symbol (at most 72), typed twice. The form ticks each rule once it's met, and shows every problem at once (a taken name comes with free names to pick, and an email that already has an account with Log in instead).
- **The email doesn't have to be real,** but you log in with it: note it down. A misspelt common address ("gmial.com") gets a "did you mean". Use a password you don't use anywhere else.
- Five accounts an hour can be made from one connection. Registering signs you straight in.
- An invite link opens the form with the name of the player who invited you (see [Invite your friends](13-events.md#invites)).

## The page's settings {#settings}

The gear at the top right (on a phone or tablet, at the top centre; F10 in full screen) opens the page's settings:

- **Display:** resolution, a frame rate limit (60 FPS unless you choose 30 to 240 or No limit; the game itself always updates 40 times a second, its ticks, the frames between ticks smoothed), FPS and ticks on screen (either, both or neither), window size, interface size, full screen, Battery saver (which also turns off light and shadows), glow effects, Light and shadows (the yard's light, the shadows everything in it casts and the Halloween cobwebs; off for a faster game on a slow device), Reduce motion (no screen shakes, nodding buildings or zooming windows; it follows your system's setting by default) and colour-blind colours (friends blue, enemies orange on the map and in chat).
- **Sound:** Volume, Music and Effects sliders (with their %), Interface clicks, Mute while this tab is hidden (on by default), and Notification sounds (off unless you turn it on: a mention, a letter or a quest ready). The sound and music buttons in the game remember your choice in this browser.
- **Browser notifications,** while the tab is hidden: your turn in a Siege duel, an outpost attacked, an alliance invite.
- **Ask before spending Shiny:** Always, or only over 10, 50 or 200.
- **Account:** change your password (your current one and the new one twice; every other device and saved login is signed out), your name or your email (with your password), forget this computer, and the **Shiny lock**.
- **Language,** **Photo mode** (a picture of your yard to download, with filters and frames), and a button that hides the settings button until you reload.

## Spending Shiny {#spending-shiny}

- The game asks before spending Shiny anywhere (the store, speed-ups, finish now, instant builds and upgrades, unlocks, healing, kits, moves, takeovers and more), and says how many you have and how many you'll have after.
- **The Shiny lock:** spending more than the amount you choose asks for your password first.
- **Not enough Shiny** says how many more you need and how many you have, lists what is ready to collect now (your Daily Reward, quests), and has a button that takes you there.

## The top bar {#top-bar}

- **Four shortcut bars** right of your Shiny: Alliances, Attack Log, Leaderboard and Change Log, with counts of what's new. When their names don't fit (a phone, a narrow window) they show as badges; the Shiny counter always keeps its number.
- **The Alerts bell** says what it holds ("2 attacks · 1 battle report") and opens one list with View for each.
- Some picture-only buttons have captions (the Club's), and a long press shows a button's tip on a touch screen. The Daily Reward's counter shows the whole day number, 10 and over too, and so does the mail's.
- The browser tab's title counts mentions and messages waiting, and says when your yard is under attack.

## The Change Log {#change-log}

- Every change ever made to the Inferno, day by day, newest first: click a day on the left to go to it. Its button counts the new days you haven't seen.
- A search box, and a click on a change's area tag (Yard, Map, Chat...) shows only that area. Entries changed or reversed later are marked so.
- It has its own page too: [/changes](/changes).

## Windows and messages {#windows}

- Windows share one close button, in the same corner, and a few have a "?" with a short how-to. The Store, the Alliance window and the kit window open on the tab you last had; the Quest Book, Leaderboards, Attack Log and Change Log shrink to fit a small window.
- Messages that pop up by themselves wait their turn, and wait until you close the Pit, an Arcade match, Hellgate Defense or the Quest Book.
- While the game shows "Please wait", a second click can't send the same thing again.
- **When something goes wrong:** going too fast says how long to wait and tries again by itself; a failure the server reported shows its reference in small print, for a bug report. Every stop has a **Reload** button that restarts the game still signed in: away too long, Connection Lost, "Anyone home?", errors, or a new version out.
- **Maintenance:** players in the game are warned 15, 5 and 1 minutes ahead; when it begins your yard is saved and you are told.
- **Inferno tips** show under the loading box while a yard loads.
