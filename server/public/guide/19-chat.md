# Chat and mail {#chat}

The chat at the bottom has two tabs, Global and Alliance, and stays usable over the Brimstone Pit, the Devil's Arcade, Hellgate Defense and the reading windows (the Quest Book and the like). Click its header to open or close it. Please keep to the [Rules](rules.md#chat).

## Reading {#reading}

- **Names** show the player's alliance tag in brackets, coloured by how your alliance stands with theirs: blue for your own, green for friendly, red for hostile, gold for the rest. A green dot before the name means they're online, grey that they aren't. [Admin] and [Mod] badges show who runs the chat; nobody else can wear them.
- **How long ago** each message was sent shows at the right: now, 33s, 2m, 4h, 3d.
- **Mentions:** a message with your name in it is highlighted in gold, and the tab shows an @. Unread messages are counted on the tab you're not looking at, and the browser tab's title counts mentions and messages waiting.
- **Reading back** doesn't pull you down when new lines arrive: a "3 new ↓" button (gold when one mentions you) jumps down.
- **Alliance news in colour:** members joining, leaving or removed, promotions, new pins on the board, power-ups and more each have their own colour. Alliance chat keeps its last 200 lines, and the board's top pin shows above the tab.
- There is no word filter: what players write shows as it was typed.
- Chat opens on the tab you used last. On a phone it starts folded the first time; folded, its button says how many players are online.

## Writing {#writing}

- Up to 200 characters, in a box that grows so you can always see your whole message.
- If a message can't be sent (too fast, or muted) the chat tells you why and keeps your message; a line typed while the chat reconnects is sent once it's back.
- In Alliance chat, a line from the leader or an officer with **@all** calls every member (and **@officers** the officers).

## Emotes {#emotes}

- Press the little devil-face button beside Say and pick one of 52 emotes (19 of them move): it's sent at once, with whatever you had typed. Shift-click to put several in one line (up to 5) before sending.
- Type **:grin:** (or any emote's name between colons) and it becomes the emote. The picker shows your recent ones first.
- On a touch screen, the first tap on an emote shows which one it is; a second tap sends it.

## A player's name {#name-menu}

Click a name in the chat, Who's Online or the leaderboards for its menu:

- the player's **card** (level, world, outposts, ranks, best Hellgate Defense run, when they started) and their tag and online state;
- **Send a message**, **View yard**, **Jump** to their yard on the map, **Find** them on the leaderboards, **Mention in chat**, **Copy name** and, for alliance leaders and officers, **Invite to my alliance**;
- **Ignore** (it also blocks their mail; unignoring lets both through again) and **Report this line**, which sends the line and the ones around it to the admins.

## Who's Online {#whos-online}

- The **Who's Online?** button on the left of the chat bar (or **/online**) lists everyone online now (active in the last 5 minutes) and everyone on in the last day, most recent first, with their alliance tags. Filters: All, My alliance, Friendly and Hostile, and a Find box for a name or tag.
- **Appear offline,** at the list's top right, keeps you off everyone else's list and off the Watch list (nobody can watch what you're doing), and greys your dot until you turn it back, even after a reload or a server restart; an Offline chip shows by the chat tabs meanwhile.

## Commands {#commands}

| Command | What it does |
|---|---|
| /help | Lists the commands |
| /ignore Name, /unignore Name, /list | Hide or show a player's messages; the players you ignore |
| /clear | Empties this tab |
| /msg Name | Writes a letter to a player seen in chat |
| /who Name | Finds them on the leaderboards |
| /online | Who's Online |
| /events | The events calendar |
| /changes | The Change Log |
| /guide, /faq, /schedule, /shiny, /rules | The help pages: this guide, questions, when things happen, Shiny, the rules |

## Mail {#mail}

- A letter that arrives while you play is announced in a short line with an Open the mail button.
- A letter can be up to 580 characters; the box counts down once 100 or fewer are left. You can send up to three replies in a row before the other player answers.
- Reporting a letter sends it to the admins and also ignores the sender.

## Moderators {#moderators}

- Moderators ([Mod]) and admins ([Admin]) can remove a message and mute a player for a while, with a reason. A muted player is told at once for how long, and why.
- How the chat is run, and how to appeal, is on the [Rules](rules.md#moderators) page.
