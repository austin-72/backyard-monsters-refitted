# Leaderboards {#leaderboards}

The Leaderboard bar at the top of the screen (the gold trophy) opens the leaderboards. Three tabs:

- **Outposts:** who holds the most outposts (ties go to the higher empire value), with their world, alliance and empire value.
- **Alliances:** the highest total empire value, with members and outposts; click an alliance to see its members.
- **Gamblers:** who has played the most Shiny in the Brimstone Pit, with their bets and overall result.

## Using the boards {#lb-use}

- **Every world, or pick one.** Every player is listed, not just the top ten. Admins aren't on the boards.
- **Find me** takes you to your own row; **Find** jumps to a player by name, or part of one.
- **Sort** by any column: click a heading (biggest first, or A to Z for names), again for the other way, and a third time, or on #, for the normal order. Ranks stay the same.
- **Click a player** for the same menu as a name in chat: send a message, jump to their yard, invite them, copy the name. **Jump** opens the map on a player's yard on your world.
- **Updated every 5 minutes.**
- The Devil's Arcade's games have boards of their own, in the Arcade (see [The Devil's Arcade](16-arcade.md#arcade-play)).
