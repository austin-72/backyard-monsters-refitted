# Alliances {#alliances}

The Alliances window was redesigned in the game's familiar beige look. Open it from the Alliances bar at the top; its button counts the invites, join requests and new pins waiting. Your alliance's chat lives in the chat's Alliance tab. An alliance has up to 50 members.

## The window {#alliance-window}

- **Along the top:** your alliance's crest, name, rank, level, leader, your role, members online, outposts and empire value.
- **Tabs:** Overview, Board, Outposts, Members, Power-Ups, Recruit (leader and officers), Invites and Browse. Without an alliance: Browse, Invites and Create.
- **Overview:** the description, Leave, the newest pins on the board, this week's outposts gained and lost, and the power-ups running with their time left.
- **Edit** (the leader): the description, the alliance's **name** (a rename is announced in alliance chat), its **chat tag** (1 to 5 letters or numbers; until one is set, the first 3 letters of the name), its **crest**, and **who can join**: open, by request or by invite only, and a lowest level.
- **Design your own crest:** Custom, first in the shield grid, opens a designer with 6 shapes, 10 patterns, 24 emblems, 4 rims and 16 colours (or Random). It's free, and shows wherever your alliance's shield does: the window, Browse, invites and the map.

## The Board {#board}

- Pinned messages with a title, a message and, if you like, a place on the map. Up to 50 at a time; each stays for 30 days. The leader can keep a pin for good, and officers can renew one for another 30 days.
- The leader and officers pin, edit, reorder and remove. A new pin is also posted in Alliance chat.
- Jump opens the map at the pinned place; "Board (2)" tells you how many new pins you haven't read yet. From the map, Share then Pin to alliance board fills the place in for you.

## Outposts history {#alliance-outposts}

- The last 90 days of outposts your members took or lost: when, who, from or to whom (and their alliance) or which tribe, where, and Jump.
- Filters: gained or lost, players or tribes, one member, one world. Totals for the last 7 and 30 days.

## Members and officers {#members}

- **Members:** role, level, outposts, empire value, when they joined, and online or last seen (members not seen for over a week are marked inactive). Sort by any column, or search. Players who appear offline don't show as online here.
- **Visit yard:** from Members, step through your mates' yards with the arrows, and go Back to Members.
- **Actions:** send a message or jump to a member's yard; the leader and officers can remove members (not the leader or other officers); the leader can make or remove officers and hand over the lead, staying on as an officer.
- **Undo a kick:** after removing a member you have 5 minutes to undo it; they come back with their role and join date. A removed player gets a letter saying who removed them.
- **Coming back within a day:** a member who leaves or is removed and rejoins within 24 hours gets their role and join date back. The Leave question says what you would lose.
- **Taking the lead:** if the leader hasn't been seen for 14 days, the longest-serving active officer can take the lead from the Overview. It never happens by itself.
- **Disbanding:** an alliance you disband is kept for 14 days, and you can bring it back from the Alliance window, as its leader.

## Recruiting and joining {#recruiting}

- **Recruit** (leader and officers): suggested players, filtered by your world, a lowest level and players active in the last day, with pages; **Invite by name** for anyone else. The sent invites are listed (who, when and by whom) and can be cancelled; an invite runs out after 14 days, and it shows who sent it.
- **Browse** ranks alliances by empire value (every member's main yard and outposts on the map), the same as the leaderboards. It finds alliances by tag or name, shows each one's tag, its members ("12/50" or Full), who can join, and how each alliance sees yours. Click an alliance's badge or name for its card: the badge large, its whole description, leader and members, and Actions.
- **Asking to join** lets you add a few words for the leader; leaders and officers see them with the request, along with your level, world, outposts and when you were last on. Joining an alliance turns down your other pending invites.
- A friend who joins the game through your invite link gets an invite into your alliance if you lead it or are an officer.

## Stances and power-ups {#powerups}

- When another alliance changes its stance toward yours, your feed says so.
- **Power-ups** recharge in 4 days. Starting one asks first, saying how long it works and how long it then takes to charge again; alliance chat says when one runs out and when it is ready again. Every hour bought to speed one up counts, even from two members at once.
