# Rules {#rules}

Inferno Map Room 2 is a small server run by players for players. These few rules keep it fair and friendly for everyone. Anything they don't cover, the admins decide, in the same spirit. The [player guide](index.md) explains how everything in the game works.

## Your account and alts {#alts}

- **One account is all you need.** You may keep a second one (an alt), but every account plays for itself.
- **Alts don't feed other accounts:** no attacking your own accounts for their loot or outposts, no passing outposts between your accounts, and no extra accounts to push a leaderboard, an alliance, the Gauntlet or the Devil's Arcade.
- **Your account is yours alone.** Don't share it or play someone else's. An account plays in one place at a time; if someone else signs in to yours, sign in again and change your password.
- **Names:** nothing hateful or obscene, and no pretending to be an admin or a moderator. An admin can rename an account.

## Invites {#invites}

- **Invite real friends.** When a friend signs up with your invite link, you both get 250 Shiny.
- **Inviting yourself doesn't pay:** nothing is paid between accounts on the same connection, and an admin can bar an account from invite rewards, and take back what it earned, for making accounts to collect them.
- Share your link where people want it. Don't flood the chat or strangers' mail with it.

## Play it yourself: no bots or macros {#automation}

- **No bots, scripts, auto-clickers, macros or programs that play for you,** no changed game, and no requests the game doesn't send. The game's own helpers are fine: AUTO in the Brimstone Pit, Collect all, Refill army, kits, the keyboard keys and the rest.
- **No keeping your yard "online" while you're away** (mouse jigglers and the like) so that it can't be attacked.
- **The server checks every request.** Anything a fair game can never send is refused and reported to the admins with its proof. No one is banned by a machine: an admin looks at every report before deciding.
- **Found a bug that gives Shiny, resources or scores?** Report it (the game's bug report, or an admin) instead of using it. Using a bug on purpose counts as cheating. Report a security problem privately to an admin, never in chat.

## Chat {#chat}

- **Be kind.** Banter and trash talk about the game are fine; harassment, threats, hate (about anyone's race, religion, gender, sexuality, disability or the like) and sexual content are not.
- **Keep private things private:** don't post anyone's personal information, and never ask for passwords or saved logins. Admins will never ask for your password.
- **No spam or flooding,** no advertising other games or servers, and no scams.
- There is no word filter here, and "hell" is fine. Keep it so anyone can read the chat.
- **Use the tools:** Ignore hides a player's chat and mail; Report this line (on a name's menu) sends the line and the ones around it to the admins, and Report on a letter sends the letter.

## Moderators and admins {#moderators}

- **Admins** ([Admin], in red) run the server. **Moderators** ([Mod], in blue) help in chat. Nobody else can wear the badges.
- Moderators can remove a message and mute a player for up to a day. Admins can mute for up to 30 days, ban an account, rename it, take back what was gained by cheating or a bug, and take scores off the boards.
- **A mute or a ban always comes with a reason,** and you are shown it. A ban lasts until an admin lifts it.
- Moderators are players too. If you disagree with a mute, don't argue it in chat: appeal instead.

## Appeals and questions {#appeals}

- **Think a mute, a ban or another decision was wrong?** Ask an admin on {{discord}}: give your account name, say what happened and why you think it was a mistake. One calm message is enough; an admin will look at it and answer.
- The same goes for questions, a forgotten password (the game has no "Forgot password" button, but an admin can help) and anything you'd rather tell an admin privately.
