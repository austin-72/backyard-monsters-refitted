# The Devil's Arcade and Hellgate Defense {#arcade}

The Devil's Arcade is a building for your main yard, cheap and quick to build: Moloch's hall of games. Sixteen cabinets, each with its own leaderboards, to play alone or against other players. It's all for glory: nothing is won or lost, your own monsters stay home, and the chat stays open while you play.

## Playing {#arcade-play}

- **Against other players:** when nobody comes, a bot or a ghost takes the other place. Players looking for a rival are announced in chat with a **Join** button, the cabinets show how many are waiting, and waiting can go on in the background, with a chip at the top to come back or leave the queue.
- **A dropped connection** in a versus or race game gives you 12 seconds to come back to the same match.
- **Leaderboards:** all 20 places, ten a page, and "Around you" when you're outside them; Solo boards rank by the best score, the others by wins. After a game the result shows your place and how far you moved up ("#7 (up 3)"). Balthazar's Flight has a This week board.
- **The dailies:** Siege's Daily (the same yard and army for everyone, three tries a day; a try that never got going is given back), Valgos Golf's Daily (the same nine holes for everyone) and Hellgate Defense 2's Daily Challenge are new at midnight UTC.
- **Siege Duels:** challenge a player (or anyone) to the same yard and army; the better attack wins. You're mailed when it's your turn, duels show how long they have left, and a finished duel has REMATCH.
- **Keys:** the arrow keys turn the lobby's pages, Esc goes back, Enter plays again from a result. P or Esc pauses a solo game (Q quits from the pause), and a solo game pauses when you switch away. Leaving a game with the X asks first; a match against another player counts as a loss.
- **Touch controls** on phones and tablets: a stick to move and buttons for the rest. They can be set left-handed, bigger or smaller, and more see-through.

## Hellgate Defense {#hellgate}

- An empty walled arena: monsters pour in on the left and march on your Hall on the right. Place your walls with the Yard Planner between waves to make their way as long as you can (it moves your towers too, keeping their upgrades), buy towers with the gold from kills and cleared waves, and upgrade each tower's damage, fire rate and range. The waves never end and keep getting harder, with a champion every 10th wave.
- **Versus:** spend gold to send monsters into your rival's arena; the last Hall standing wins. **Co-op:** whatever gets through your maze runs on into your partner's, with 20 lives shared; if your partner leaves or drops, you play on alone.
- **Speed and Pause:** the speed button goes 1x, 2x, 3x, and a solo run has Pause.
- **Keys:** Space starts the wave, F changes the speed, P pauses, Tab picks the next tower, Q W E R upgrade it and S sells it.
- Point at an upgrade to see what changes ("Damage 250 → 350"). A tower sold before the next wave gives back everything you paid for it since the last wave; after a wave it sells at 70%. Selling an upgraded or expensive tower asks first.
- Every wave you clear is saved as you go: a run cut short by a reload is recorded at the last wave cleared.

## Hellgate Defense 2 {#hellgate2}

- **Four maps:** The Gate, Twin Gates (two ways in), Brimstone Ravine and Moloch's Crossroads (the Hall in the middle, monsters from every corner). Reach wave 15 to open the next.
- **Twelve towers,** six of them Hellforged: the Chillstone Obelisk, Venom Spire, War Drum, Tithe Shrine, Bone Catapult and Gargoyle Roost. Every tower has a fourth upgrade, a Special of its own, and towers rank up as they kill.
- **Spells:** Meteor, Frost Nova, Blood Pact and Moloch's Wrath, on keys 1 to 4. They charge while waves are on.
- **Elites and bosses:** monsters come Armored, Swift, Regenerating, Splitting, Warded or Mending; champions have powers, and wave 40 brings the Hellgate Warden. Kill the Warden to win a map on Easy, Normal, Hard or Inferno and earn its skulls, then keep going as long as you last.
- **The Hellforge:** every run earns embers. Spend them on perks for every run after: more gold, walls and lives, cheaper towers, faster spells and more. Once a week you can reset your perks and get all their embers back.
- The end of a run shows the waves you held, the towers still standing and your MVP tower, with a Hellforge button.
