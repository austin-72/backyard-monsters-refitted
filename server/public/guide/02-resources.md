# Resources, timers and prices {#resources}

Everything moves faster than in the stock game.

| | Stock game | Here |
|---|---|---|
| Starting resources | stock | 60,000 bone, coal and sulfur; 55,000 magma |
| Starting Shiny | stock | 2,000 to 2,025 |
| Resource production | x1 | x2 (magma x4) |
| Building, upgrading, fortifying, repairing, Strongbox unlocks and Academy training | as listed | 4 times faster |
| Hatching a monster | takes a while | 1 second |
| Workers | stock | 5 in your main yard (outposts 2) |
| Storage each captured outpost adds | 2,000,000 | 5,000,000 of each resource |
| Alliance power-up recharge | 5 to 7 days | 4 days |
| Chat message length | 100 characters | 200 characters |

## Prices {#prices}

- **Shiny prices are the server's own:** packing, protection, more housing, tower overdrive, moving yards, capturing an outpost, alliance power-ups, kits, wall upgrades, resource top-ups, repair all, "finish now" and speed-ups. The [Shiny page](/shiny) lists every one, read from the server's own settings.
- **Free with 10 minutes or less left.** Anything with 10 minutes or less to go finishes for nothing: the worker under the building, and the building's own panel, say "Free" (Finish, Free).
- **Speeding up a building** (building, upgrading, fortifying or repairing it): under an hour, 1 Shiny at 11 minutes left, one more every 6 minutes, up to 9 at 59 minutes. An hour or more: 10 Shiny for the first hour and 7.5 for each hour after.
- **Damage protection:** 48 Shiny for a day, 108 for 7 days, 168 for 28 days. It adds to any you already have, and ends as soon as you attack anyone.
- **Moving your main yard:** onto one of your own outposts, 1,500 Shiny; onto an alliance member's outpost when they invite you, 150 Shiny; either can be paid with resources instead. The Map Room's Relocate (a new place picked for you) costs no Shiny (see [Moving your yard](07-map.md#moving)).
- **Repair all** on a damaged building's panel starts every repair at once, for free. When every repair is already under way it's still there, and finishes them all this instant: the Shiny "Repair all buildings" (free when they're nearly done).
- **Recycling and cancelling** say what you get back before you confirm: recycling a harvester banks what it holds, and cancelling an upgrade gives back its whole cost (the time spent is lost).

## Seeing your resources {#seeing-resources}

- Hover a resource in the top bar to see what your harvesters hold and when the storage will be full; a full storage shows FULL on its bar.
- Click a resource's bar to bank it from every harvester, or press C to bank them all. On an outpost, whose harvesters can't be banked, the bar's tip leaves out what they hold.
- From a billion, the bars show short numbers ("2.34B"); the bar's tip gives the exact amount.
