# Play in your browser {#browser}

The whole game runs in your browser, with nothing to install, and plays like the Flash game. Open the site and play: <https://inferno.maproom2.com/>

## Computers, phones and tablets {#devices}

- **Full screen:** one button works the same everywhere; zoom and chat work in full screen, and on a computer a gear appears near the top for the settings.
- **Phones and tablets:** the game fills the screen, touch works as the mouse, two fingers pan, a double tap zooms in and out, a pinch magnifies, the phone's keyboard comes up for typing (an email keyboard for emails, a number pad for numbers, Send on the chat's Enter key), and you can add it to your home screen. Opened from the home screen without a connection, it shows a small page that tries again by itself.
- **Bigger targets on touch screens:** taller menu rows, bigger attack and Pit buttons, a tap just beside a small button finds it, and lists scroll with a finger. The screen stays on during attacks, replays, Hellgate Defense, the Arcade and the Pit.
- **Always up to date:** when a new version is out the game asks you to reload.

## Quick to start, smooth to play {#speed}

- The game comes as a few small files your browser keeps, so later visits download almost nothing until the next update. Sounds load after your yard (and not at all while muted), and the loading screen shows real progress and says when the connection is slow.
- A busy yard repaints only what moved, and the map, the Yard Planner, the arcade games and the Pit draw much less each frame than they did, so all of them run smoother on slower computers and phones.
- A game left in a background tab stays quiet and light.
- If the game can't reach the server as it starts, it shows Try again, says when you appear to be offline (and tries again by itself when you're back), and has a link to copy the details for a bug report.
