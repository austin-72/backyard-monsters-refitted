# Battles and the Catapult {#battles}

## Who you can attack {#who}

- A yard must be in your Flinger's range from your main yard or one of your outposts; upgrade the Flinger, or attack from an outpost nearer to it. A yard on another world is always out of reach.
- A player's yard can't be attacked while it is under damage protection, while another player is attacking it, or, for a main yard, while its player is online.
- **Damage protection:** a main yard is protected for 36 hours after an attack leaves it 50% or more damaged, and for 1 hour after 4 attacks within an hour; an outpost for 8 hours after an attack leaves it 25% or more damaged. Attacking anyone, wild yards included, ends your main yard's protection at once; the game names the yards that may lose it, with their coordinates, before you go.
- **Keep monsters at home:** when attacking from the map, Yards lets you keep any of your yards' monsters at home.

## In the attack {#attacking}

- **Loading the Flinger:** each monster in the attack bar has **All**, which puts every one of that kind in the Flinger (then None takes them all out). Shift-click "+" fills the Flinger with one kind, Shift-click "-" takes them all out, and R loads it again as it was for your last fling. The bar shows the Flinger's load in numbers ("Flinger 1,240 / 1,600"), and each monster's tip says how much room it takes.
- **Keys:** 1 to 9 put a monster from that row into the Flinger (hold Shift to fill it), Space flings where the pointer is, and Esc empties the Flinger when nothing else is open to close.
- **The 90% line:** every attack on an outpost or a tribe's yard shows the destruction bar with its 90% line, as Moloch's Gauntlet does. The counters show loot taken and what's still lootable.
- Hover a tower while scouting or attacking to see its range.
- A champion's Retreat button sits apart from Send; on a touch screen it is held for half a second.
- **End Attack** asks first while monsters and time are left.

## After the battle {#after}

- The result offers the next step: **Take over now** after flattening a tribe's yard or an outpost, **Attack again** after a failed attack, or **Back to the map**, with **Home** beside it. After an attack on a player: **Watch replay** and **Share in chat**.
- A short fanfare plays for a win and a crash for a loss; after a raid on your yard, a sound says whether you held it off.

## Catapult ammunition {#catapult}

The Catapult (main yard, from Under Hall 3) fires the Inferno's own ammunition. Four sizes of each; size 2 needs Catapult level 2 and so on. One shot of each kind per attack. Esc puts an aimed bomb away, Q aims the last one again.

- **Marilyn Monstroe:** lures the defenders' monsters to her, then explodes for 500 / 1,500 / 4,000 / 8,000 damage. Costs 100k / 500k / 2.5M / 5M magma and half as much sulfur.
- **Candy Jars:** every tower in range is trapped in a jar for 15 / 25 / 40 / 55 seconds and its shots hit the glass. The glass cracks, shakes, then breaks. Costs 100k / 500k / 2M / 5M bone and coal.
- **Sulfur Bomb** (was Putty Rage): your monsters are faster the whole time, can't be hurt at all for the first 0 / 4 / 8 / 12 seconds, then take much less damage until it wears off. They glow red, orange, then yellow. Costs 100k / 500k / 5M / 10M sulfur.

## Battles that behave {#fixes}

- Shielded monsters can die: small hits add up properly, and Quake Towers hit through a shield as they should.
- The Blast Tower hits the Candy Jar like every other tower instead of firing through it.
- Balthazar doesn't set traps off, and Quake Towers ignore him. Walls stop him, though: he goes round them or breaks through, as walking monsters do.
- Sulfur Bombs reach burrowed monsters.
- Rezghul raises only his own side's fallen monsters.
- Compound defenders all come out at ground attackers, even while the Compound is being built.
