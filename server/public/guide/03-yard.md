# Your yard and your workers {#yard}

## Warts {#warts}

- **Warts instead of mushrooms.** Crimson, lava-cracked warts grow in your main yard by themselves: one about every 4.8 hours, up to 20 at a time, offline time included. A worker picks them, and about one in four is a golden wart worth 3 or 8 Shiny.
- **Pick all warts:** the wart button in the workers' column sends your idle workers to every wart in the yard, one after another, with one summary of the golden ones at the end.
- Every weekend the **Wart Bloom** makes them grow three times as fast (see [Events and rewards](13-events.md#wart-bloom)).

## Your workers {#workers}

- **5 workers in your main yard**, 2 in each outpost. They are always out and about, wandering between jobs.
- **Each busy worker shows the time left** under it, and "Free" once the job can be finished for nothing: click it to finish. Clicking a busy worker opens the building it works on; an idle one opens the build menu.
- When every worker is busy, the game names the job that ends soonest, how long it has left and what finishing it costs.
- **Command your workers** (main yard): click one to make it dance, tell a joke (they know plenty), wave, follow you or go for a stroll.
- **Sacrifice a worker to Moloch:** an idle worker can be given to Moloch for 500 Shiny. It is gone for good (you are warned first), and you always keep at least one. Changed your mind? You can take it back within 2 minutes, as long as you still have the Shiny it earned.
- The D.A.V.E. Club lets you pick Underworld imps or Overworld builders as your workers (see [The D.A.V.E. Club](17-club.md)).

## Light and shadows {#light}

- **Every yard is lit from the evening side.** Everything standing in it casts its own shadow away from the light: buildings and decorations, every monster and champion (in a battle of more than 30 monsters, just their round shadows), workers and pets (pets also have a soft shadow under their feet). Moving parts cast moving shadows.
- **For a faster game on a slow device,** turn off **Light and shadows** in the settings, under Display. Battery saver turns it off too. It also turns off the Halloween cobwebs (see [Events and rewards](13-events.md#cobwebs)).

## Moving around your yard {#moving-around}

- **Zoom:** the mouse wheel zooms in towards the pointer, up to three times; turn it back to normal size and on again to step out to the zoomed-out view. On a touch screen, spread two fingers to magnify and pinch to go back; on a trackpad, two fingers scroll and a pinch zooms. The zoom button takes you straight back to normal size, and zooming keeps a building you're placing or moving.
- **Keys** (when no window is open and you're not typing): the arrows or W A S D move the view, + and - zoom, H goes to the Under Hall, B opens Buildings, P the Yard Planner, T the Store, M the map, Q the Quest Book, C banks every harvester and F goes full screen. Press ? for the list.
- **Esc** cancels placing or moving a building, or closes the window on top. The browser's (or Android's) Back button does the same; with nothing open it asks "Leave the Inferno?" instead of closing the game. **Enter** closes a message that only tells you something (never one that would spend or recycle).
- Clicking the dark area around the Change Log, Leaderboards, Attack Log, Quest Book, Who's Online, Outposts or Alliances window closes it.

## Placing and moving buildings {#placing}

- **Press and hold** a building to pick it up and move it, as the Move button does.
- Dropping a building where it can't go keeps it in your hand and says so, with **Put it back**.
- A tower's range ring follows it while you place or move it.
- Opening the Quest Book or the Store while placing a new building doesn't drop it: it is back in your hand when the window closes.
- On a touch screen, a building being placed sits above your finger, with ✓ and ✕ to place or cancel.
- A short sound plays when a building or an upgrade finishes while you are in the yard.

## Attacked while you play {#attacked-live}

- **Your yard can be attacked while you play.** When another player attacks it, your game switches to the attack as it happens: it plays out over your yard as it was when the attack began, with who is attacking, how long it has gone on and how much is destroyed.
- **Nothing in the yard can be done until it's over:** the yard is the attacker's for the length of the attack, so anything you were doing that wasn't saved yet is not kept. The **Map Room** button opens the map; closing it takes you back to the attack.
- When the attack is over, **Return home** gives you your yard back as the attack left it.
- **Somewhere else** (an outpost, the map): you're told who is attacking, with **Watch**. An attack on one of your outposts can be watched the same way.
- The map opens even when your Map Room has been wrecked.

## Visiting someone's yard {#visiting}

- A panel shows the owner: alliance, stance, coordinates, and online or last seen (click it for their menu), with **Bookmark**, **Share**, **Open on map**, **Home** and **Attack** when it's in reach.
- Click a building for its card: level, health, a tower's range and damage, a storage's rough loot. The panel also gives a rough idea of what destroying the whole yard would pay.
- Signs with a message are marked and show it on hover; clicking someone's pet shows its name and kind.
- **Copy layout** puts the yard's buildings (no traps or decorations) in your Yard Planner's Import.
- The top bar keeps Alliances, the Attack Log, Watch, the Leaderboards and the Change Log while you visit.

## Away from the keyboard {#away}

- After 10 idle minutes the game stops and asks "Anyone home?". A minute before, a soft chime and "Still there?" give you a chance to move; typing in chat, the keyboard and the mouse all count as being there. While the Brimstone Pit or the Devil's Arcade is open you are never marked away.
- When the game has stopped, moving the mouse, pressing a key or coming back to the tab reloads it by itself, still signed in.
- If the server stops answering, the game says "Not saved yet, trying again", then "Reconnecting…", and carries on by itself once the server answers. Closing the tab right after doing something doesn't lose it: the waiting save is sent at once.
