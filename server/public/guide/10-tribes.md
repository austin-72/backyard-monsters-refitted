# Tribes, Moloch and wild attacks {#tribes}

The four overworld tribes became devil tribes living in Inferno yards, and Moloch joined as a fifth.

## The tribes {#tribe-list}

| Was | Now | Yard levels |
|---|---|---|
| Legionnaire | Hellionnaire | 25, 29, 33, 37, 41 |
| Kozu | Kozmodeus | 30, 34, 38, 42 |
| Abunakki | Abaddonakki | 27, 31, 35, 39, 43 |
| Dreadnaut | Beelzenaut | 28, 32, 36, 40, 44 |
| (new) | Moloch | 46 and 50 (strongholds) |

- **Territories.** Every piece of land belongs to a tribe, in territories around a seat where the levels are highest. A world's tribes never move.
- **Tribe yards are Inferno yards:** Compounds, bone walls, Blast, Quake and Magma Towers and booby traps instead of the overworld's buildings, turned half round from the overworld layouts you may know.
- **Tribe yards heal by themselves:** 12 hours after their last attack a tribe or Moloch yard is fresh again, on the map too.
- New pictures for all five tribes, on the map, in popups, at the top while you view or attack them, and in the attack warning.

## Moloch {#moloch}

- **Strongholds:** about 310 per world, at level 46 or 50, never where a new player lands. Moloch also rules the Depths of Hell (see [The Depths of Hell](09-depths.md)).
- **The loot:** a stronghold holds 60 million each of bone, coal and sulfur and 30 million magma at level 46, and twice that at level 50, but you carry off only a share: its Under Hall gives a part of what is left and each Resource Pod a smaller part, and magma pays half. Clearing a whole stronghold pays roughly 12 to 14 million each of bone, coal and sulfur and 3.5 to 4 million magma at level 46, and about 23 million each and 8 million magma at level 50.

## Wild monster attacks {#wild-attacks}

- **At most one a day** (every 23 hours), on your main yard only, only while you're playing, never in your first minute after signing in, from yard level 3.
- **Who comes:** any of the four tribes, or Moloch now and then (1 in 20). Each sends a set army for your level (1-10, 11-20, 21-30, 31-40, 41+).
- **The warning** shows the strongest three kinds of monster and "+N more", the raid's level, and when it arrives in your own time and in how long. An arrow at the edge of the screen points to where it will land, the countdown's last 30 seconds pulse and its last five tick.
- **Time to get ready:** a raid that came due while you were away from home (in an outpost, the Gauntlet or someone's yard) waits two minutes after you get home, and warns you again.
- **After the raid,** a window says what it cost: buildings lost, walls broken, traps set off and the resources taken, with Re-arm All if traps went off. The raid is in your Attack Log under Attacks on me, with a replay.
