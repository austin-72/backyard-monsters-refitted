# Outposts {#outposts}

Take a tribe's yard or another player's outpost and it is yours: each one adds 5,000,000 of storage for every resource to your main yard.

## The Outposts list {#outposts-list}

Open it from the outposts counter's button at the top of the screen.

- **Every outpost** with its position, name, value, bone / coal / sulfur / magma per hour, protection left, monsters, how full its Compound is ("120/200"), busy incubators ("1/2"), **Idle** workers, **Towers** (how many and the lowest fortification) and **Damage** ("Attacked!" while an attack is under way, its damage in red from 50%). Sort by any column; View visits it, Map shows it.
- An outpost you haven't opened yet still shows what it makes, marked as an estimate.
- **Names:** give an outpost a short name (click its X, Y or Value). The name shows in the list, on the map and in the map's sidebar.
- **Needs attention only** keeps the list to outposts with something to do, and the arrows follow it. Depths outposts next to a portal are marked in purple.
- When a storage at home is full, the list says which and how much your outposts are wasting an hour.
- **Heal all** heals every outpost's defenders still wounded from an attack, without visiting each one (outposts under attack are left alone).
- Smooth even with 1,500 outposts.

## Getting around {#outposts-around}

- **Previous, Home and Next:** step through your outposts in the order the Outposts list was last sorted, round and round. The top bar's outposts counter shows which one you're in ("12/40"). Home takes you back to your main yard.
- A yard opened from the Outposts list offers a way back to it.
- While you're online, an outpost that is attacked, wrecked or taken shows a notice (with the horn) and a badge on the Outposts button.

## Workers and defenders {#outposts-workers}

- An outpost has two workers, shown at the top as in your main yard: it can build or upgrade two things at once. On the map an idle worker stands beside the outpost, and a second one behind it when both are idle.
- **Defenders remember the last attack:** Compound defenders and Monster Bunker losses stay as the last attack left them until you visit the outpost (or use Heal all), which heals them.

## Kits {#kits}

- **Kits are built at once,** at their full levels, whether you pay with resources or with Shiny: nothing is left to build up. Kits 1 to 6 (Ember to Apocalypse) cost 50 to 1,000 Shiny bought outright.
- **Your own kits:** save up to six of your outposts' layouts, rename or delete them, then build one in another outpost for resources (no Shiny). A saved kit keeps its earlier version for 7 days.
- In the kit window, each kit's rows are green where it has more or higher than what the outpost has built, and red where it has less.
- Applying a kit sends the outpost's housed monsters home (the warning says how many).
- **Take over and build a kit:** the takeover window can build one of your kits on the new outpost right away, paid with resources. Tick **Stay on the map** to keep the map open for the next one.
- **One kit on several outposts:** Select in the Outposts list ticks outposts to build one kit on all of them, with the total price asked first.

## Sending monsters {#transfers}

- When sending monsters, choose the target outpost from a list showing its free room; + and All stop at that room. **All** (then None) moves every monster, and Shift-clicking + or - moves every monster of that kind.
- Monsters move between your own yards and outposts only.

## Letting one go {#abandon}

- An outpost can be let go on its own from its map popup, after typing ABANDON. The rest of your outposts stay.
- Relocating your main yard lets every outpost go (see [Moving your yard](07-map.md#moving)).
