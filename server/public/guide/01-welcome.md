# Welcome to the Inferno {#welcome}

Inferno Map Room 2 is a custom Backyard Monsters Refitted server where everyone lives in the Inferno. Your yard, your outposts and the whole world map are Inferno, from your first minute. It runs in your browser, with nothing to install (see [Play in your browser](23-browser.md)).

## Everyone is in the Inferno {#everyone-inferno}

- **Your main yard is your Inferno yard.** Lava ground, the Inferno buildings, bone, coal, sulfur and magma, the Inferno monsters and the stone-and-fire look, in your main yard and in every outpost. There is no separate Inferno yard to travel to.
- **The world map is an Inferno world.** Instead of the overworld tribes you meet devil tribes and Moloch's strongholds (see [Tribes](10-tribes.md)).
- **Coordinates are plain numbers.** A world goes from 0, 0 to 399, 399, and the Depths of Hell below it from 0, 0 to 9, 9.

## Starting out {#starting-out}

- **No tutorial.** You start straight in the Inferno with 5 workers (an outpost has 2). A new player is welcomed once and shown the Quest Book's first quest; the quest dock shows the Getting Started quests first until they're done.
- **A head start:** 2,000 to 2,025 Shiny, 60,000 each of bone, coal and sulfur and 55,000 magma (see [Resources, timers and prices](02-resources.md)).
- **Where you land:** within 20 cells of another player, but never right next to a main yard or a Moloch stronghold.
- **Damage protection** for your first 7 days.
- **No Discord check** to sign in, and no welcome popup or invite popup each time you sign in.
- The Quest Book's first quests walk you through the basics: a Sharpshooter, the Compound, an Incubator, your first monster, a Flinger and your first fling.

## Shiny {#shiny}

- **Shiny is never sold here.** You earn it: the Daily Reward, the Quest Book's quests and chests, golden warts, inviting friends, Moloch's Gauntlet, sacrificing an idle worker to Moloch, and the Brimstone Pit's games. When you are short, the game says how many more you need and what is ready to collect, instead of offering a shop.
- There is no monthly 500 Shiny: the Daily Reward replaces it.
- Every Shiny price, and how much each way of earning pays, is on the [Shiny page](/shiny).
- The game asks before spending Shiny (you can make it ask only over 10, 50 or 200), and an optional Shiny lock asks for your password above an amount you choose (see [Signing in, settings and the interface](22-interface.md#spending-shiny)).

## Help in the game {#help}

- The **Quest Book** (the Quests button) is a tour of everything there is to do, with Go there buttons (see [The Quest Book](14-quests.md)).
- The **Change Log** (the top bar) lists every change, day by day.
- In chat, **/help** lists the commands, and **/guide**, **/faq**, **/schedule**, **/shiny** and **/changes** open the help pages.
- Type **/events** in chat for a calendar of every event and daily reset, in your own time.
