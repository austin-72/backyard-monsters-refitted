# The D.A.V.E. Club and looks {#club}

The D.A.V.E. Club's button, under your resources, opens the choices for how your yard and your name look. Everyone who visits or attacks your yard sees what you picked. The Store's **Looks** tab leads to the same skins, emotes, alliance crests and animated decorations.

## Ground {#grounds}

- Pick the ground your yard stands on: every ground there is, from the Inferno's and the overworld's to the outposts'. It shows in your yard behind the Club at once, and goes back if you close without saving.
- Three are opened by inviting friends: **Ashfall**, its embers glowing (1 friend), **Obsidian Glass**, fire shifting through its seams (2), and the **Soulfire Rift**, its blue rivers flowing (3).
- Five more are opened by how many outposts you hold: **Sulfur Springs**, its springs bubbling (50), the **Magma Sea**, its lava shifting between the plates (100), the **Witchfire Marsh**, green fire flickering in its pools (250), **Bloodstone**, its veins beating (500), and the **Gilded Inferno**, its rivers of white-gold fire (1,000).

## Your name in chat {#name-colour}

- Pick one of twelve colours for your name in chat, for everyone to see. Only the name is coloured.
- **Name effects,** for players who invite friends: **Ember Glow**, shaded red to gold (1 friend), **Brimstone**, a light sweeping across it (2), and **Soulfire**, its colours flowing all the time (3).

## Workers {#worker-looks}

- Choose the Underworld imps or the Overworld builders as your workers, in all your yards.

## Signs {#signs}

- Place up to 10 free Signs in your yard and write a message of up to 80 letters on each. Anyone looking at your yard can point at or click a sign to read it.

## Champion skins {#skins}

- Five skins for every one of your champions: **Molten**, **Void**, **Gilded**, **Bone** and **Venom**. Pick a champion in the Club's Champions tab to see them walking before you buy.
- Each is 400 Shiny and yours for good; switch between the looks you own at any time. Your champions wear them at home, in your attacks and for everyone who visits.

## Emotes {#club-emotes}

- 52 emotes for the chat, 19 of them animated, free for everyone. The Club's Emotes tab shows them all (see [Chat](19-chat.md#emotes)).

## Trophies and pets {#club-tabs}

- **Trophies:** what you've won, how to win the rest, and Place in yard (see [Trophies](13-events.md#trophies)).
- **Pets:** your pets, with a one-step swap of a stored pet for one that's out (see [Pets](18-pets.md)).
