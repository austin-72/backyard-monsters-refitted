# The world map {#map}

Worlds are 400 x 400 cells with up to 625 players. The map room window was rebuilt, and you can see the whole world at once. Its "?" opens help in short pages you can flip through.

## The map room window {#map-window}

- **Five zoom steps:** World, World 2x, World 4x, Far and Close. Use the - / + buttons, the mouse wheel or pinch. It reopens on the step you left it on, and on a big enough window it grows to fill it.
- **Filters:** choose whose yards show (you, your alliance, friendly, hostile, other alliances, no alliance), main yards and outposts, your Flinger range and your bookmarks. The **Targets** column narrows it to a level range, hides protected or locked yards, or keeps only yards in your Flinger's range; yards left out are faded, or hidden if you prefer.
- **Sidebar:** Home (with a Depths button under it), Jump, **Find a player** (everyone on your world, with their main yard and outposts, each with Go), **bookmarks**, your alliance's members on the world, your **Outposts** (each with Go) and the **Portals** (both ends of each).
- **Bookmarks:** as many as you like, renamed and removed at will. Each shows the owner or tribe, protection or damage, the distance and whether it's in reach; sort them, and give each a colour by clicking its pin.
- **Back** returns to where you were before each jump, and **Recent** lists the last 5 places you looked at.
- **Go to a cell:** type coordinates such as 123,45 in the search and press Enter.
- **Targets:** a button listing every yard within your reach (type, level, damage, protection, distance), sortable, with Go and Attack.
- **Right panel:** a minimap of the whole world (click or drag to move), and everything about the place under your pointer: owner and level, what it is, alliance, standing, protection and location.
- **Keys:** the arrows or W A S D move, + and - zoom, H goes home, B bookmarks the place in the middle, and F puts the cursor in Find.
- **Share a place in chat:** from an empty place or a yard's popup, post it to Global or Alliance chat; anyone can click it to open the map there. Leaders and officers can pin it to the alliance board too.

## A yard's popup {#popups}

- A protected yard's popup says how long its protection lasts ("Protected: 5h 12m"); a damaged tribe yard says when it resets.
- A tribe yard's or outpost's popup says what taking it over would cost and whether you're short, or that you can't hold more outposts.
- Before an attack leaves the map, the game checks the yard can be attacked right now; if it can't (damage protection, someone else attacking it), you're told why there. A player who is online can be attacked: they watch it as it happens (see [Attacked while you play](03-yard.md#attacked-live)).
- A yard you've looked at repeats the rough loot its whole yard would give.
- Your own outposts' popups show their production, monsters housed, idle workers, protection, towers and damage (see [Outposts](08-outposts.md)).

## The whole world at once {#whole-world}

- The three widest zoom steps show the whole world: every player's main yard and outposts as coloured dots. Hover a dot to see who it is, click to go there.
- Damaged tribe yards show orange, and those ready to take over red, on the world map and the minimap (a Filters switch hides them).
- Light purple dots are the portals down to the Depths of Hell (see [The Depths of Hell](09-depths.md)).
- **No truces.** There are none in the game: no truce requests, no truce icons on the map, and no yard is kept from attack by one.

## Inferno ground {#ground}

- The map is drawn as the Inferno: lava seas, bone fields along the shores, netherrack darkening as it rises, and black rock with glowing magma at the peaks. Each kind of ground has four looks, so the map never looks tiled.
- Yards on the map are drawn as the Under Hall and the Inferno outpost hall, and free outposts have the Inferno worker beside them.
- Outposts and wild yards stand on their own ground, matching the height of their place on the map: bone fields by the shore, darker rock higher up. Main yards stay on lava (or the ground you picked in the D.A.V.E. Club).

## Moving your yard {#moving}

- **Relocate** (on the Map Room building's own buttons, or your own yard's popup on the map) moves your main yard to a new place on any world with room, picked for you as a new player's is. It costs no Shiny, but your bone, coal, sulfur and magma go to 0 and every outpost returns to the wild; the warning counts what you'd lose, monsters housed in your outposts included, and you type RELOCATE to confirm.
- **Onto one of your own outposts:** 1,500 Shiny or resources. It asks a second time, with Keep as the safe answer, since the outpost and its buildings are destroyed. Not onto an outpost in the Depths, and not again until 24 hours after such a move: a greyed Move button says why and how long is left.
- **Relocation invites:** from one of your outposts, invite an alliance member to move their main yard onto it (150 Shiny or resources for them). Moving to another world warns how many outposts you would lose. Revoking an invite asks first.
- None of these moves can happen while your yard is being attacked.
- Your bookmarks stay when your main yard moves within the same world.
