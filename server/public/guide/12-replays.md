# Attack Logs and replays {#replays}

The Attack Log bar at the top of the screen (the crossed swords) opens your Attack Logs. Its button counts the attacks on you that you haven't seen yet, and it opens on Attacks on me when there are new ones.

## Attack Logs {#attack-logs}

- **Two tabs:** My attacks and Attacks on me: when, who (or which tribe), the yard, the damage and the loot. The Loot column shows the main resource and how many others ("1.2M Bone +2").
- **Filters** by yard type and by won or lost, the last 24 hours' attacks and loot, and older attacks loaded past the first 60.
- **Repeated attacks grouped:** the same attack over and over in a row (the same tribe yard, say) is one line, "Name ×6", with the best damage and the loot added up. Click the name to see each attack.
- **Report** opens the details: the exact day and time, the yard and where it is, the damage, the buildings knocked down, the loot taken or lost, and the battle log.
- **Jump** opens the map on the yard; **Revenge**, on an attack on you by a player, opens the map on the attacker's yard.
- Wild tribes' raids on your yard are listed too, with what was lost and a replay.
- Under the list: "Refreshing…" while a new one is asked for, then "Updated 14:02" in your own time.
- **Welcome back:** signing in after attacks shows one card: who attacked which yard, the damage and loot lost (with Replay and Revenge), damaged buildings (Repair all), injured monsters (Heal all), full harvesters (Bank all) and rewards waiting. Two or more attackers while you were away are listed in one window.

## Replays {#watching}

- **Every attack between players is recorded,** on main yards and outposts, and so are wild tribes' raids on your yard. The attacker and the defender can both watch: Replay in the Attack Logs, then Watch the replay, or Watch replay right after the attack.
- **What you see:** the yard as it was, the monsters moving and fighting, the buildings taking their damage, the Catapult's shots and the monsters' glows, with the battle's music and sounds.
- **Controls:** Pause and Play, ¼x, ½x, 1x, 2x and 4x, Restart, Share, Save and Close, and a **seek bar** with the attack's damage drawn along it and marks where catapult shots landed: click or drag it to jump to any moment.
- **Share** it in chat, Global or Alliance ("Ann vs Bob · 87%"): anyone who clicks it can watch. Share can also copy a link that opens the game straight into the replay.
- **Download** it to watch later, even after it's gone from the server: Open a replay file, in the Attack Logs, plays it again.
- **Kept for 7 days;** each player keeps at least the 10 newest attacks on them, however old. Press **Keep** on a replay to keep it for good (up to 10).
- Closing a replay you opened from the Attack Log takes you back to the log, where you were.
- A very large attack's replay is marked "cut short" if parts of it couldn't be recorded.

## Watching live {#watch-live}

- **Watch,** on the top bar between the Attack Log and the Leaderboards, lists everything going on right now, the longest first: who is attacking whom (a player's yard or outpost, a tribe, the Gauntlet, Siege), and who is playing in the Brimstone Pit, the Devil's Arcade or Hellgate Defense, with how long it has been going.
- **Click a name to follow that player:** you watch whatever they're doing, and whatever they do next. While they do nothing that can be watched, you wait for them.
- **Attacks** play out over the yard as they happen, a few seconds behind, with who is attacking what, how long it has gone on and how much is destroyed. **Games** show the player's screen as sharp as they see it, up to 15 times a second (fewer on a slow connection), with their chat covered, in a viewer as big as your screen allows.
- **Watch all** shows the attack that has been going the longest, then anything else that's live, and waits for the next thing to start when nothing is. **Next** skips to something else.
- **Light on data:** between a whole picture now and then, only the parts of the screen that changed are sent, and nothing while it's still.
- **Players appearing offline** (Who's Online) aren't listed and can't be watched.
- **Being watched:** while anyone watches you (your game, or an attack you're making), a small list at the top right of your screen says who. Players appearing offline are counted, not named.
- **Stop watching** takes you home. Opening another yard stops it too.
