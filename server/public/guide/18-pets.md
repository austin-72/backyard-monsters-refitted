# Pets {#pets}

Pets are small copies of the Inferno's monsters that wander your main yard. They're just for looks: nothing fights them and they fight nothing.

## Getting pets {#getting-pets}

- **Buildings > Decorations > Pets** (or the D.A.V.E. Club's Pets tab): a card for each monster, with Buy, Out, Store and Name.
- Every Inferno monster but the champions can be a pet, for 500 Shiny each.
- **Up to 5 out at once;** the others wait in storage. Out brings one into your yard, Store puts one away. Up to 5 of one monster, and up to 60 in all.
- **Changed your mind?** A pet never fed can be returned for its full price within 10 minutes of buying it, or released later for half.
- **Names:** Name gives each one a name (up to 16 letters), shown over it in your yard. Leave it empty for no name.
- Only in your main yard, never in outposts. Visitors and attackers see your pets too, and clicking someone's pet shows its name and kind.

## Playing with your pet {#playing}

- **Click your pet** for a menu of things to do together: have it follow your cursor, play fetch, do a trick, get a pat, run zoomies or take a nap, with hearts, sparkles and bones to show it's having fun.
- **Full size:** feed your pet 1,000,000 magma and it grows to a monster's full size for good. You are asked first, because it can't be undone.
