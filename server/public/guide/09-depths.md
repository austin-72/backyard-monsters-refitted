# The Depths of Hell {#depths}

Below the world map lie the Depths of Hell: a land of 10 x 10 cells, one per world and shared by everyone on it. Its portals join places far apart on the map, so it's mostly a way to travel.

## Portals {#portals}

- **About ten portals on every world,** at the centres of the biggest lava pools. They never move, and nobody can attack or take them.
- **Each portal comes out below at its place on the map divided by 40:** a portal near 160, 100 comes out at Depths of Hell 4, 2. Places down there go from 0 to 9 each way, as the world above goes from 0 to 399.
- **Light purple dots** show every portal on the zoomed-out world map and on the minimap. Click one on the world map to go straight down through it. The map's sidebar lists both ends of every portal (Portals), and Depths, under Home, takes you down.
- **Click a portal** to see where it comes out. Anyone can press Enter the Depths of Hell to go down and look. To attack down there, one of your yards needs that portal in its Flinger range, or you need an outpost below already. A portal you can't attack through yet names your nearest yard, how far it is and how far that yard's Flinger reaches. Go up, on a portal below, always works.

## The land {#depths-land}

- An island in a lava sea: every cell is a platform of black and red brimstone, standing apart from the others with lava between them and a wide plank bridge to each neighbour. Four kinds of platform: cracked crust over molten rock, red-hot brimstone cobbles, a burning seal, and fire vents.
- **Neutral ground:** every cell is +0% for resources and +0% for defence (the world above has more or less, by height).
- In the Depths the minimap shows its 10 x 10 platforms, outposts, damaged camps and portals; click to go there.

## Moloch's Depths {#depths-moloch}

- Every cell is a Moloch stronghold until a player takes it: levels 38, 42, 46 and 50, the bases of the old descent (6 to 13). The easiest are next to the portals, the hardest furthest away.
- Taking one over costs twice as much, in resources or in Shiny. Kits cost the same as anywhere.
- Strongholds heal 12 hours after their last attack, like every tribe yard.

## Range {#depths-range}

- **Going down:** a yard of yours with a portal in its Flinger range can attack the cells next to where that portal comes out below.
- **In the Depths:** an outpost there has no Flinger. It always reaches the cells next to it (Declare War doesn't add to that), and flings as many monsters as your main yard's Flinger can.
- **Coming back up:** an outpost of yours next to a portal below can attack anything within 5 cells of where that portal comes out on the world map.
- **Free and instant:** going through a portal costs nothing and has no wait.

## Rules down there {#depths-rules}

- Outposts in the Depths work like any outpost: resources, housing, protection and attacks. The Outposts list numbers them D0 to D9, and the Location boxes show them as, for example, D4 x D5.
- Main yards can't move there, and nobody can be invited to move onto an outpost in the Depths.
- An outpost in the Depths goes back to Moloch only if its owner leaves the world; otherwise another player can take it, like any outpost. It counts on the leaderboards like any outpost.
- On the map, a banner says you're in the Depths of Hell, the map doesn't zoom out past Far there, and Home brings you back up.
