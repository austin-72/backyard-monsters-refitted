Inferno Map Room 2

Inferno Map Room 2 is a Backyard Monsters Refitted server set in the Inferno, playable in your browser with no Flash or download.

What you can do

  • Build a hellish yard of Inferno buildings and towers, and defend it with walls, traps and monsters.
  • Raise Inferno monsters and champions, and raid other yards on Map Room 2, a shared world map of the Inferno.
  • Take over yards as outposts, and join an alliance with other players.
  • Play the Devil's Arcade, a building full of minigames with leaderboards, from Spurtz Smash to Hellgate Defense.
  • Try your luck in the Brimstone Pit with Shiny, and work through the quest book.
